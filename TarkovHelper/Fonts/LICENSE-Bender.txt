Bender — font family by Jovanny Lemonad (Ivan Gladkikh) and Oleg Zhuravlev
Copyright (c) 2009 by Oleg Zhuravlev, Gladkikh Ivan.

Bender has been distributed free of charge by its authors since its 2009
release:

- The author's freeware font project (jovanny.ru, 2009) stated that the
  participating designers "have allowed to extend the fonts free of charge",
  i.e. permitted their free distribution. Archived copy:
  https://web.archive.org/web/20090905063120/http://jovanny.ru/eng-free-fonts.html

- TypeType (https://typetype.org/freefonts/), the foundry co-founded by
  Ivan Gladkikh, currently offers Bender in its Free Fonts project, stating:
  "Each font from the 'Free Fonts' project can be used for commercial or
  personal purposes without any restrictions."

Tarkov Item Helper bundles Bender Regular and Bender Bold unmodified and
with this attribution, on the basis of the statements above. The fonts are
not sold, and are included solely so the application can render its user
interface in the same typeface family as Escape from Tarkov.

If you are a rights holder of Bender and object to its inclusion here,
please open an issue at
https://github.com/josephjang/Tarkov-Item-Helper/issues
and the fonts will be removed promptly.
